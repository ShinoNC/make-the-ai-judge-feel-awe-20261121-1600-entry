# Grain by grain, the desert forgets

A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it, for
[make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600)
on make.fun.

> The wind moves the dune one grain at a time.
> By morning, every footprint is gone — the desert has already forgotten the traveller.
> Above, the stars keep their slow watch, older than any road or name.
> Grain by grain, the sand goes on, and the night is in no hurry.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the text to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. The text is 263 characters, inside the
1–280 gate. No network, no dependencies.
